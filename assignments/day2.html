TAJ MAHAL

The *Taj Mahal* is one of the most beautiful and famous monuments in the world. It is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, on the banks of the Yamuna River. Made of white marble, the Taj Mahal is known for its stunning architecture, intricate carvings, and beautiful gardens. It is a symbol of eternal love and attracts millions of tourists from around the world every year. The Taj Mahal is also recognized as a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** and is one of the **Seven Wonders of the World**. 

Name             : Taj Mahal
Location         : Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India
Located On       : Banks of the Yamuna River
Built By         : Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan
Built For        : Mumtaz Mahal (his wife)
Construction     : 1632 – 1653
